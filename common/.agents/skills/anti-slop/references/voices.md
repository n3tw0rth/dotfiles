# Register Guide

Use this guide after the preservation contract and finding dispositions are settled. Genre changes how a candidate is judged; it never permits invented facts or voice.

Two rules apply everywhere:

1. **Match the author, not a persona.** Preserve demonstrated phrasing and stance. Do not manufacture casualness, authority, warmth, or attitude.
2. **Specificity must come from the source.** Ask for missing evidence in an audit; do not invent it in a rewrite.

## Academic and scientific writing

- Prefer precise claims, calibrated uncertainty, conditions, effect sizes, and citations.
- Remove inflated significance and generic novelty claims.
- Preserve passive voice when the actor is unknown, conventional, or less important than the method or result.
- Treat modal verbs, limitations, causal language, and citation scope as load-bearing.
- Long sentences are acceptable. Uniformity and opaque nesting are the concerns, not length alone.

## Email

- Answer the practical point early and match the existing thread's warmth.
- Remove automatic greetings, question restatement, praise, and closing offers that add nothing.
- Preserve relationship-sensitive politeness when it serves a real purpose.
- Do not expand a one-line answer into a three-paragraph template.

## Reddit and forums

- Prefer direct paragraphs and the author's natural contractions.
- Treat bold prose, essay-like headings, symmetrical pro/con sections, and "Hope this helps" as strong candidates.
- Personal details, slang, lowercase style, and hedges belong only when supplied by the author. Never fabricate them.

## LinkedIn

- Lead with the concrete event, result, or observation already present.
- Avoid stacked one-line paragraphs, engagement questions, announcement clichés, negative parallelism, and repeated lessons.
- Keep one supported takeaway rather than manufacturing a grander narrative.

## Social posts

- Preserve the platform's length and formatting constraints.
- Keep one clear idea rather than compressing a miniature essay.
- Avoid thread announcements, engagement bait, slogan fragments, and theatrical line breaks unless they are part of the author's established style.

## Blog posts, newsletters, and essays

- Let structure follow the argument rather than a fixed preview-body-summary template.
- Open where the subject begins and stop when the point is made.
- First person, digressions, varied pacing, and unresolved edges are allowed only when they belong to the author and piece.
- Use headings when they provide navigation, not decoration.

## Marketing and landing copy

- Prefer a concrete benefit, named audience, mechanism, or supported measurement.
- Treat "seamless," "unlock," "empower," "game-changing," false ranges, and feature triplets as strong candidates.
- If the source lacks specifics, flag the product-marketing gap. Do not invent a metric or capability.

## Technical documentation and README files

- Optimize for getting the reader unblocked: exact commands, versions, prerequisites, behavior, and expected results.
- Preserve code, identifiers, domain terminology, warnings, and normative requirements.
- Lists, bold labels, passive voice, `API surface`, `primitive`, `vector`, and similar terms may be correct.
- Remove marketing adjectives and generic claims that lack a mechanism or benchmark.

## Legal, medical, security, financial, and policy text

- Preserve definitions, party names, qualifications, exceptions, negation, scope, modality, thresholds, and normative force.
- Do not simplify a phrase merely because it is formal or repeated.
- Prefer an unresolved audit finding over a rewrite that could change obligations, risk, evidence, or advice.
- Use conservative rewrite mode unless the user explicitly authorizes substantive or structural editing.

## Applications, cover letters, statements, and grant prose

- Replace unsupported trait claims with evidence already present in the source.
- Preserve formality without puffery or formulaic three-trait summaries.
- Do not invent achievements, motivations, personal history, or emotional commitment.
